Confidentiality & Client Trust

Privacy Protocol & Data Governance

Our commitment to discrete fiduciary client representation, secure cloud architecture, and strict non-disclosure.

1. Fiduciary Commitment to Discretion

At GCC Luxury Real Estate LLC, client confidentiality and discretion form the bedrock of our boutique brokerage practice. Whether you are acquiring a signature waterfront residence or subscribing to our Private Register for off-market opportunities, your personal and corporate details are held under strict fiduciary standards.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly provided by you via our digital consultation and inquiry channels, which may include:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full legal name, email address, and direct telephone/WhatsApp number.
  • Acquisition Preferences: Preferred regional markets (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha), target square footage, and investment criteria.
  • Inquiry Notes: Specific property questions, viewing schedule requests, or portfolio structuring requirements.

3. How Your Information Is Used

All collected data is utilized exclusively for the following authorized purposes:

  • Assigning a dedicated senior advisor to coordinate private on-site viewings and property walkthroughs.
  • Delivering verified architectural floor plans, developer dossiers, and financial models.
  • Transmitting confidential off-market notifications if enrolled in our Private Register.
Zero Commercial Monetization: We never sell, rent, lease, or trade client information to third-party marketing firms, advertisers, or unaffiliated aggregators.

4. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

To maintain high availability and end-to-end encryption in transit and at rest, our lead submission workflows utilize Supabase (PostgreSQL cloud database infrastructure with Row-Level Security policies). All transmissions use modern TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. Data Retention & Rights

You retain the right to request access to your contact record, request amendments, or request complete removal from our advisory databases and newsletter registers at any time.
